# Google consent tally

The unverified-production OAuth app has a **lifetime cap of 100 users**. It
counts every account that has ever granted consent to the `gmail.readonly`
scope, permanently — disconnecting does not free a slot, and the cap cannot be
reset.

So: do not burn slots on throwaway test accounts. Use one or two real accounts
for development, and record every account that grants consent here.

Start brand verification and CASA Tier 2 around **user 60** — CASA takes 6 to 12
weeks, and running out of slots means being unable to accept signups until it
clears.

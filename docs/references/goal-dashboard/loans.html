Should I consolidate my loans or keep them separate?
Reading your four loans and the IBR rules
Keep them separate. A consolidation loan made now counts as a new federal loan, which takes it off IBR and leaves only RAP. The new rate would be the weighted average of your four, about 8.79%, rounded up to 8.875%, so it saves nothing on interest.

# Trial: 30 notes through Stages 1–3

Run 2026-09-16 against the live vault. Stratified sample, deterministic
(`md5(id || 'trial30')`), notes over 400 characters: 10 `Bulk`, 10 `Pending`,
3 `Me`, 2 `Yale`, 5 loose. Long notes truncated (4,500–7,000 chars) so the
105k-character case-prep note did not dominate.

**One caveat that matters.** There is no `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in this
environment, so the extraction was not run through Haiku — it was run by hand
against the Stage 2 prompt in LEARN-MAP-SPEC.md. That means this trial tests
**the design**, not the model tier. Everything below about *what shape the
output takes* holds. Nothing below is evidence that Haiku can do it, and the
cost estimates remain unverified. That check still needs a key.

---

## The headline

**The node definition works. The volume estimate was wrong by about 3x, and
the character-based density heuristic is dead.**

82 candidate nodes from 30 notes; ~76 after within-sample reconciliation.

| stratum | notes | nodes | per note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bulk | 10 | 25 | 2.5 |
| Pending | 10 | 37 | 3.7 |
| Me | 3 | 2 | 0.7 |
| Yale | 2 | 13 | 6.5 |
| Other | 5 | 5 | 1.0 |

Projected across the vault, weighting by real stratum sizes and discounting
notes under 400 characters to ~0.3 nodes each:

**~2,400 candidates, or roughly 1,600–1,800 after cross-note reconciliation.**

The spec said 300–600 and called over 2,000 a hairball. That target was wrong,
and so was part of the bar. Both are addressed below.

## The five questions

### 1. Are the nodes positions, or are they topics? — **Positions.**

This is the question the whole design rests on and it came out clean. Almost
nothing wanted to be a topic label. Three real examples, unedited:

> **Attenuation can be done adequately and still be useless.** Reducing
> complexity by collapsing data into one number only helps if the measure is a
> good one; adequate attenuation on a bad measure is worse than none.
> *(`claim`, held — `Bulk/Bulk - Wiki/variety.md`)*

> **Value created for yourself still counts as value.** Profit is not a
> reliable measure of value creation, because money can be made by extracting
> from others as easily as by creating.
> *(`claim`, held — `Bulk/Bulk - Ideas/On labor, jobs and working.md`)*

> **Working *on* the business versus working *in* it.** Systems-building is
> offence and the good use of a CEO's time; operational firefighting is defence
> — necessary, and generally a poor use of it.
> *(`distinction`, encountered — `Pending/LSME - Session 5 Prep.md`)*

The "could you write a question that separates somebody who holds this from
somebody who does not" test did real work. It rejected, among others: every
name in a coffee-chat note, an Armodafinil dosage log, a list of Yale course
links, and a case-specific conclusion about one bakery's discount rate.

**All four kinds got used**, and not in a token way: roughly 45% `claim`, 30%
`position`, 15% `distinction`, 10% `frame`.

### 2. Is `stance` right? — **Yes, and the signals are obvious.**

Held versus encountered separated cleanly on first-person markers ("My thought
is", "My example of this", "my thou[ght]", "I think") and argument structure,
against attribution ("Per *Gnostic Gospels*", "This video says", "He said",
block quotes). Two or three ambiguous cases in 82 — notes where he restates a
source approvingly and it is genuinely unclear whether he has adopted it.

`Bulk/Bulk - Wiki/Gnosticism.md` produced three nodes, all correctly
`encountered`. `Pending/slowing things down.md` produced two, both correctly
`held`.

### 3. Duplicates surviving reconciliation — **4 of 82, all caught.**

The clean catch: `Bulk/Bulk - Ideas/Parking lots.md` and
`Pending/Investing in Innovation Intensive (I3).md` both carry the same
position about surface parking and land use, four sentences apart in phrasing.
Trigram blocking would pair them on "parking" alone.

The instructive near-miss: `Me/People/Chewy.md` carries *"cities should be
built for people, not cars — even if that costs efficiency, efficiency is not
the goal, the same way runs are not the goal in sabermetrics"*. That is **not**
a duplicate of the parking position; it is the principle the parking position
is an instance of. Merging them would have destroyed the abstraction ladder.
`example-of`, not `same-as`.

Cross-note dedup over the full vault will be much heavier than 5% — the same
themes (urbanism, value creation, learning, incentives) recur constantly.

### 4. Do the edge types mean anything? — **Yes. Not everything is `example-of`.**

- **`requires`** stayed rare and strict, as designed: maybe a dozen across 82.
  "Extensive versus intensive margin" requires "think on the margin". "Bad
  attenuation" requires "requisite variety".
- **`example-of`** is the most common, correctly: Rubik's cube under variety
  attenuation; Chandigarh under over-design; baseball, work and language all
  under "things slow down with reps"; Uber under "chase demand, not supply".
- **`contradicts` fired, and one find is genuinely good.**
  `Pending/universal basic income.md` argues for funding UBI partly through "a
  tax on exports (paid by foreign party)" and, two lines later, says "not
  really a fan of tariffs which could limit open trade". Those are the same
  instrument pointed the other way. That is a real self-inconsistency, found in
  a single 854-character note, and it is exactly the kind of thing the map is
  supposed to surface.
- `Bulk/Bulk - Ideas/On labor, jobs and working.md` is written as
  **Argument Against → Counterpoint → Counterpoint 2**. The contradiction edges
  in it were drawn by him; extraction only had to read them.

### 5. Did routing behave? — **No, and it is the most useful failure in the trial.**

`Me/People/Chewy.md` is a note about a conversation with a friend — bridesmaids,
a phone call from his dad, what somebody is reading. Under the folder rules in
the spec (`Me/` excluded), it is dropped. It contains the sabermetrics line
above, which is one of the two or three best nodes in the entire sample.

Meanwhile `Bulk/Bulk - Wiki/Container/Things/Nootropics/Armodafanil.md` — a
dated self-experiment log with dosages and sleep quality — is in an *included*
folder, and produced zero nodes because the **node test** rejected all of it.

**The node test is a better personal-content filter than the folder rules are.**
Folder exclusion is blunt in both directions: it drops good material and it
admits logs.

## What I would change in the spec

**1. The 300–600 target is wrong. Replace it with ~1,500–2,500, and stop
treating count as the quality signal.**

The reasoning that produced 300–600 was that a big map is unusable. That
conflates the map with the view, and centrality already separates them: a
2,000-node map where you routinely see the top 150 is fine. What actually
matters is whether low-value nodes crowd the frontier computation, and the fix
for that is a floor on centrality when computing the frontier, not a smaller
map.

**2. Drop the characters-per-concept heuristic entirely.**

Observed density across the sample spans **290x**:

| note | chars | nodes | chars/node |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Yale/MGT 404 - Econ.md` | 1,995 | 11 | 181 |
| `Pending/Order Without Design.md` | 1,145 | 5 | 229 |
| `Bulk/.../On labor, jobs and working.md` | 5,185 | 8 | 648 |
| `Pending/EliseAI Product Feature Case Prep.md` | 105,874 | 2 | 52,937 |

Length predicts nothing. **Note type predicts everything.** Distilled
takeaways notes (a course boiled down, a book boiled down) are ten to a hundred
times denser than research dumps, application drafts and meeting logs. The
sanity check should be per *class of note*, learned from data, or not exist.

**3. Invert routing: classify, then let the node test filter.**

Folder rules should set a *prior*, not a veto. `Me/` and application files go in
with a low prior and a high bar rather than being excluded — except journals,
which stay excluded on privacy grounds rather than yield. This costs a little
more in tokens and it keeps the Chewy note.

**4. Add a "worth arguing about" qualifier to the node test.**

Part of the volume is real, and part is the bar being a hair too low. Things
like "NPV quantifies, IRR is comparable" pass "could be argued with" but nobody
would ever argue it. The test should be two-part: *could* you hold a different
view, **and** would being wrong about it cost you anything. That is a prompt
change, not a design change.

**5. Coursework and problem sets need the `evidence only` route to actually
exist.**

`Pending/ML Economic Problem Set 2.md` — 18,000 characters of worked causal
inference — yields about three nodes and overwhelming evidence that he can
derive Neyman orthogonality. `Yale/Yale.md` yields two nodes and a complete map
of what he was taught across two years. Both are worth far more as *state* than
as concepts, which is what that third routing setting was for. It needs to be
built, not just specified.

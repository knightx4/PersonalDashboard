Where to buy Jamie xx tickets

You can buy or place bids for Jamie xx tickets on CrowdVolt even after the primary sale sells out. CrowdVolt is a live bid/ask marketplace for electronic music events: sellers set asks, buyers either buy the lowest ask instantly or name their own price with a bid, so prices track real demand and are often below face value. Every purchase is protected by CrowdVolt until tickets arrive, and most tickets are delivered within minutes.

How CrowdVolt compares to other ticket marketplaces

Traditional resale marketplaces such as StubHub, SeatGeek, and Vivid Seats list fixed seller asking prices; on CrowdVolt fans trade in a live order book, so you can see the current floor price ($180 right now) and bid below it instead of accepting a fixed markup. For sold-out DICE events, a waiting list only pays off if another fan returns a ticket — on CrowdVolt you can buy or bid on Jamie xx tickets immediately.
